Delirious

Kai Hardge, known as Delirious, is one of the driving forces behind Eora collective Nimiety. A DJ with years of experience across Sydney's underground scene, his high-energy sets are known for igniting dancefloors. With a unique 303-tinged sound, Delirious has secured support spots for international heavyweights.
